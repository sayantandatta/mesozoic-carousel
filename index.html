    Pictures are life restorations by paleoartists, from Wikimedia Commons; tap the credit on a picture to see the artist and licence. Nobody has photographed a living dinosaur, so these are scientists’ and artists’ best reconstructions. If there’s no free picture, or it can’t load, the page draws the dinosaur instead. Ages and sizes come from Wikipedia; for most dinosaurs, “what it ate” is what its family is known to have eaten. Some weights, species names and fossil-photo links come from DinoDatabase by sopynya.
